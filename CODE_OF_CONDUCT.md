# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

We are committed to a respectful, welcoming, and harassment-free community. Be considerate, discuss ideas in good faith, accept constructive feedback, and respect differing backgrounds and experience levels.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, sustained disruption, publishing another person's private information, or retaliation against someone who raises a concern.

## Scope

This standard applies in project spaces and when someone is representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this standard and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Enforcement should be proportionate, private where possible, and respectful of the safety of everyone involved.

The repository owner must configure a private conduct-reporting contact before publication. Do not send conduct reports through public issues. This unresolved contact is a publication blocker.

## Attribution

This policy is informed by widely used open-source community conduct standards and is intentionally concise for this project.
